How it works

Open your mailbox in 3 steps

Come in during office hours, or apply from anywhere in the world by mail.

Bring two IDs

One photo ID, such as a driver's license, state ID, passport or alien registration card, plus a second ID such as:

  • a utility bill in your name
  • a health, medical or insurance card
  • a military, school or club ID

Complete two forms

USPS Form 1583 and the American Post Box application, signed.

Download the forms [TBD: host PDFs on new site]

Get it notarized & start receiving mail

Applying in person? Our notary is on site. Applying remotely? Have a notary (or a US consulate or embassy abroad) verify your forms and IDs, then mail them to us. We open your account and assign your box number.

FAQ

Questions, answered

What would my address be?

Your Name or Business Name

Inglewood, CA 90302, USA

Is this a P.O. box?

No. It's a private mailbox at a real street address, so you can receive packages from every carrier, not just USPS.

Can you forward my mail somewhere else?

Yes, anywhere. We send it by US Priority Mail unless you tell us otherwise, and you set the terms. Forwarding is a $3.50 service charge plus postage.

Do I need to live in California or the USA?

No. You don't need to be a US or California resident to use our address to receive mail in the Los Angeles area.

What's the minimum term?

USPS regulations require at least 3 months for a new account. Longer prepaid terms cost less per month.

How do I check my mail without coming in?

Use our call-in service. Set a password on your account file (it can't be set over the phone), then call to hear what's arrived, authorize a one-time pickup, or arrange forwarding.

Can someone else pick up my mail?

Yes, if they're listed on your application, or if you authorize a one-time pickup by phone using your password. Valid ID is required to claim box contents.

What happens when I close my box?

Your exit fee covers forwarding. Once you tell us your closing date, we hold mail received up to 90 days after closing and forward it to your new address.

Why are your rates so low?

Low overhead, plain and simple. Customers stay for the personal service.
